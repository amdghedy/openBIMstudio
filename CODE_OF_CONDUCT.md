# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior: empathy, respect for differing views, constructive feedback, accountability, community focus.

Unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, harassment, publishing private information, other unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
